About Me

Hi, I'm Syed Ashruf, a Physical Design Engineer at Semiconos, where I joined on 16 November 2026. I take digital blocks from RTL to GDSII on 28 nm and 40 nm, covering synthesis, floorplanning, placement, CTS, routing and signoff checks in Synopsys DC and ICC2 and in Cadence Genus and Innovus, with timing in PrimeTime and Tempus and equivalence checks in Formality and Conformal. I have worked on designs of up to 380k standard cells and 32 macros, closing timing and fixing congestion, DRC and connectivity issues. At Semiconos I completed a GPIO block and am testing an AI-driven dashboard for physical design flows. Earlier I did research on tunnel FET biosensors with published papers, and I served as Chairperson of the IEEE Electron Devices Society student branch at KL University.

Physical Design Projects

AI-Driven Flow Dashboard (Semiconos)

Implementation and sign-off flow tracking
Testing an AI-driven dashboard that shows each RTL-to-signoff stage and its checks, and compares Synopsys runs against Cadence runs.

Tile

28 nm | 9 metal layers | ~380K cells | 588.24 MHz | 18 macros
RTL to GDSII with block-level signoff. Logic-aware synthesis with clock gating and a restricted multi-Vt strategy, utilisation sweeps to size the block, manual setup fixes after placement, and clean-up of opens, shorts and floating nets.

Iguana SoC

28 nm | 9 metal layers | 411,860 cells | up to 90.9 MHz | 32 SRAM macros | 8 clocks
RTL to GDSII with 8 clocks across 3 PVT corners. Cleared placement hotspots, closed setup violations by resizing cells and adding buffers, and fixed opens, shorts and floating nets.

CVA6

28 nm | 9 metal layers | ~288K cells | 1.5 ns clock period (~667 MHz) | 24 macros
Full RTL-to-GDSII flow on a block with about 900 input ports. Optimised port placement for routability, debugged congestion in dense regions, and resolved port buffering and unloaded-port issues.

RP_TOP

28 nm | 9 metal layers | ~68K cells | 526.32 MHz | 6 SRAM macros | 1 clock
Rectilinear floorplan, full power grid, CCD-enabled CTS with double-width double-spacing clock NDRs, and timing-driven SI-aware routing. Final routing had zero DRCs and zero open nets; post-route hold fixed with timing ECOs.

SSOC200 (CX_CPHY_RX_X1)

40 nm ULP | 7 metal layers | 10,850 cells | up to 200 MHz | 1 analog hard macro | 16 clocks
Mixed-signal CPHY receiver block of SSOC200 (also covering the CSS and PSS blocks). Floorplan at 60% utilisation around the analog macro, then placement, CTS and routing closed with zero setup and hold violations. Applied OCV derates, LVT/ELVT cell rules, XOR clock generator balancing and routing resistance limits.

Testblock: RTL Half-Cycle Paths

28 nm | synthesis only (no routing layers) | 488 cells | 1 GHz main clock | no macros | 4 clocks
RTL to gate-level netlist (rtl_halfcycle_crosspaths) with Synopsys DC. Four clocks (1 GHz, 454 MHz, 232 MHz and a divide-by-2 generated clock) constrained as asynchronous groups, with half-cycle paths analysed and setup/hold violations resolved.
